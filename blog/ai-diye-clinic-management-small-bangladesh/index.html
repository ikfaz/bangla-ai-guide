Dr. Tahmid-এর 6 AI Use Cases

1. Appointment + Reception Automation

WhatsApp Business + ManyChat + ChatGPT API integration:

  • Patient WhatsApp-এ "appointment" message → auto-reply with available slot।
  • Confirm slot → Google Calendar block।
  • Day-before reminder SMS auto-send।
  • No-show track + follow-up template।

Receptionist time saved 3 hrs/day।

2. Patient Records (EMR Lite)

Consultation শেষে voice note (Bangla, 30-60 sec) → ChatGPT/Claude Vision OCR-aided transcription → SOAP format note → Google Sheet patient record।

"নিচের voice transcript থেকে SOAP-format note বানাও:
S (Subjective): Patient-এর complaint, history।
O (Objective): Vitals, examination findings।
A (Assessment): Diagnosis (provisional/final)।
P (Plan): Investigation, medication, follow-up।
Patient-identifier anonymize। Bangla-English mixed OK।"

3. Prescription Generation

"Patient 45-yr-old male, BMI 28, BP 150/95, fasting glucose 7.2 mmol/L। Current medication: Metformin 500mg BD। Lifestyle: sedentary, smoker। Prescribe:
(১) Hypertension management — first-line + dose,
(২) Diabetes control optimization,
(৩) Lifestyle counseling specific to BD context,
(৪) Follow-up timeline + lab test।"

AI suggests, doctor verifies + signs। Faster than manual writing।

4. Billing + Invoice Automation

  • Service catalog + tariff in Google Sheet।
  • Patient visit completed → AI generates invoice (Bangla, NBR-compliant)।
  • bKash + cash + insurance claim integration।
  • Monthly P&L auto-summary।

5. Follow-up + Patient Retention

"নিচের patient list (anonymized): age, condition, last visit, treatment plan। Identify priority follow-up needed। Generate Bangla WhatsApp template for each — appointment reminder, lab test recall, lifestyle check-in।"

Retention rate 41% → 78% (Dr. Tahmid's measured)।

6. Insurance Claim Processing

  • Pragati Insurance, Reliance, Green Delta — BD insurance claim form auto-fill।
  • Medical justification narrative AI-drafted।
  • Supporting document checklist auto-generate।

BD Small Clinic AI Tool Stack

  • Google Workspace ($6/user/mo): Sheet (records), Calendar (appointment), Docs (prescription template), Drive (file storage)।
  • WhatsApp Business + ManyChat ($15/mo): Patient comms automation।
  • ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo): SOAP note, prescription, follow-up।
  • Otter.ai ($8/mo): Voice transcription।
  • Optional: Local EMR (eHospital, Doctor Pro) ৳৫-১৫K/mo: Comprehensive solution।
  • Total monthly: $50-80 (~৳৬K-১০K)।

BMDC Compliance + Patient Privacy

  • Patient identifier: Never paste to public AI। Anonymize (Patient-A, Patient-1)।
  • Consent for AI-aided care: ২০২৫-এর BMDC guideline — patient consent for any AI-aided documentation।
  • Data retention: 5+ years প্রতি patient। Local backup mandatory।
  • Insurance fraud red flag: AI suggestion-এ blindly insurance claim submit risky। Manual verify।

৪টা Mistake

  • 1. Pure paper records: 2026-এ unsustainable। Patient search impossible।
  • 2. No follow-up system: 60% revenue from repeat patient। Tracking-হীন = revenue loss।
  • 3. AI prescription unverified: Drug interaction + dose AI-mistake patient-harm potential। Doctor verification mandatory।
  • 4. Privacy breach: Patient name AI-পাঠানো = BMDC violation + lawsuit risk।

উপসংহার — আপনার আজকের পদক্ষেপ

Dr. Tahmid বলেন: "ছোট clinic-এ AI আমাকে patient-এর জন্য বেশি সময় দিতে enable করেছে। Admin task automate, doctor's bandwidth medical care-এ। আমার patient experience improved, revenue উঠেছে, এবং personal time আমি family-এর সাথে কাটাই — Sunday-আর সব-আগের-paperwork ছিল না।" আজই করুন: যদি আপনি clinic চালান — WhatsApp Business install করুন এবং ManyChat-এ basic appointment auto-reply set up করুন (90 minutes one-time)। আগামী সপ্তাহে আপনি receptionist-এর 5+ hours back পাবেন। Patient-side experience instantly upgrade।
