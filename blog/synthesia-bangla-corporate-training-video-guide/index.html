প্রথম ভিডিও বানানো (Robi corporate context)

  1. Sign in: Synthesia Enterprise dashboard (corporate SSO via Microsoft 365)
  2. Template select: "Compliance Training" template → BD anti-fraud training
  3. Avatar: Pre-built corporate presenter বা custom (Mahfuza created — 30 min studio recording → AI clone in 48 hours)
  4. Script: Compliance officer-এর review-করা Bangla + English version
  5. Slide integration: PowerPoint upload → synchronized with avatar narration
  6. Branding: Robi logo, color palette, ending CTA — brand kit apply
  7. Compliance review: Legal team-এর approval workflow (Synthesia commenting feature)
  8. Generate: 15-30 min processing for 10-min module
  9. SCORM export: Robi-এর internal LMS (SAP SuccessFactors)-এ direct upload
  10. Versioning: Compliance update-এর সময় script edit + regenerate (full re-shoot এড়ানো)

  • Creator $৬৪/মাস: 30 min, custom avatars, brand kit।
  • Enterprise (custom $৫০০-৫,০০০+/মাস): Robi-এর tier। Unlimited minutes, custom avatars, SCORM, SSO, dedicated CSM, white-labeling।
  • BD payment: Enterprise contracts via wire transfer। Smaller plans via USD card।
  • Robi's annual contract: ~$৪২,০০০ (vs traditional production ৳১.৪ কোটি/বছর) — 95% saving।

বাংলাদেশি কোম্পানির জন্য use case

1. Bank/financial services (Brac Bank, EBL, City Bank)

Compliance training (anti-money laundering, KYC, BBP), product knowledge updates, customer service standards। SCORM + LMS integration critical।

2. Telecom (Robi, Grameenphone, Banglalink)

Retailer training (12,000+ outlets), new product launches (5G, IoT), customer experience standards। Multi-language deliverable।

3. Pharma (Beximco Pharma, Square, Incepta, Renata)

Medical representative training, product detailing scripts, compliance (DGDA, regulatory)। Updates frequent — versioning advantage।

4. RMG export (Beximco, DBL, Hameem)

Production floor safety training, quality standards, buyer compliance (Walmart, H&M)। ৫০,০০০+ worker base।

5. NGO + development sector (BRAC, Grameen, ASA)

Field staff training across rural sites। Bangla + tribal language version। Offline LMS deployable।

6. Corporate onboarding (any 500+ employee company)

New hire orientation, company policy, culture videos। Same module per new hire (no need to repeat in-person session)।

টিপস

  • Bangla quality: Built-in TTS Bangla acceptable-but-stiff। Custom voice clone (own L&D specialist's voice) recommended।
  • Script test: Bangla translation cross-check native speaker-এ। AI translation কখনো subtle errors।
  • Avatar diversity: Multiple avatars (different ethnicity, age, gender) — engagement diversity-এর জন্য।
  • Module length: Corporate microlearning trend — 3-5 min module preferred (vs 30 min lecture)। Engagement higher।
  • SCORM testing: LMS-এ upload-এর আগে preview। Sync issues sometimes।
  • Compliance disclosure: "This video uses AI-generated presenter" — best practice, transparent।
