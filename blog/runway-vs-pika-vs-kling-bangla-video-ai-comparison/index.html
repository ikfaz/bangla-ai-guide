Runway vs Pika vs Kling — সেরা AI Video Generator কোনটি?

ই
ইকফাজ প্রতিষ্ঠাতা ও সম্পাদক

বাংলা AI গাইডের প্রতিষ্ঠাতা। বাংলাদেশি ব্যবহারকারীদের জন্য AI টুলস কিউরেট ও বিশ্লেষণ করেন — কোনটা BD থেকে কাজ করে, কোনটায় bKash চলে, কোনটা আসলে দৈনন্দিন কাজে লাগে।

প্রকাশিত: ২৬ মে, ২০২৬

ভূমিকা — Dhanmondi-র creative director-এর 3-way test

ধানমন্ডির একটা creative agency "Boishakhi Creative" — Senior Video Producer তাহরিম আহমেদ — ২৯ বছর, NSU Film & Media ২০১৯, ৬ বছর video production (Daraz, Lazada, Banglalink campaigns)। ২০২৬ Q1-এ একটা client brief: 8টা product-launch promotional video, total budget $২,৮০০, 3-week deadline। Traditional production শিকার $৬,০০০+। তাহরিম 3টা AI video tools-এ same brief test করলেন — Runway Gen-4, Pika 2.0, Kling 2.0। ৩ সপ্তাহ ধরে real client work-এ A/B test, peer reviewers + client feedback। ফলাফল: এক tool clear winner নয় — task-specific। Photorealism → Kling। Creative effects → Pika। Cinematic motion + editing tools → Runway। তাহরিমের new workflow: hybrid stack monthly $৭৭ — তিনটাই subscribe। এই গাইড সেই honest test-এর result।

এই গাইডে যা থাকবে: তিনটি tool-এর এক বাক্য পরিচয়, head-to-head test (photorealism/creative effects/speed), BD use case সুপারিশ, এক নজরে সিদ্ধান্ত।

Runway ML — industry standard

Runway (২০১৮ founded, New York) — AI-first video creation platform। Gen-1 → Gen-4 evolution। Hollywood-grade work (Everything Everywhere All at Once, Late Night with Seth Meyers)। Full creative suite: image-to-video, motion brush, camera control, green screen, inpainting।

  • Strength: Cinematic motion, editing tools, professional polish
  • Price: $১৫-৭৬/মাস (Standard, Pro, Unlimited)
  • Best for: Professional video producers, ad agency, film/TV

Pika Labs — creator playground

Pika (২০২৩, Stanford founders) — creator-friendly AI video tool। Pika 1.0 → 2.0 (২০২৫-এ launched)। Discord-native heritage now web-app। Strong on creative effects (Pikaffects), stylized animation।

  • Strength: Creative effects, stylized output, fun playground
  • Price: Free + $১০-৬০/মাস
  • Best for: Creators, social media, stylized art

Kling AI — photorealism leader

Kling (Kuaishou-এর Chinese product, ২০২৪ launch) — photorealism leader globally ২০২৬ Q1-এ। Long video (up to 2 min) capability। Strong motion physics।

  • Strength: Photorealism, physics, long video, character consistency
  • Price: Free tier + $৭-৩৫/মাস
  • Best for: Realistic product shots, character animation, long videos

Head-to-head: photorealism

Test prompt: "A traditional Bengali wedding ceremony, bride applying turmeric, golden afternoon light, photorealistic, slow motion"
  • Kling 2.0: Outstanding photorealism, skin textures lifelike, motion natural। Peer rating: 4.8/5
  • Runway Gen-4: Cinematic quality but slightly less realistic skin। Rating: 4.4/5
  • Pika 2.0: More stylized, less photorealistic। Rating: 3.6/5

Winner: Kling decisive।

Head-to-head: creative effects

Test prompt: "A product (smartphone) transforming into liquid metal, abstract motion, futuristic vibe"
  • Pika 2.0: Stunning creative effect, Pikaffects library deep। Rating: 4.7/5
  • Runway: Good creative output, motion brush precise। Rating: 4.5/5
  • Kling: Photorealistic but less creative flair। Rating: 3.8/5

Winner: Pika।

Head-to-head: speed

  • Pika 2.0: 6-sec clip in 30-45 seconds। Fastest।
  • Kling: 10-sec clip in 2-3 minutes।
  • Runway Gen-4: 10-sec clip in 3-5 minutes। Slowest but highest quality।

বাংলাদেশি use case সুপারিশ

1. Ad agency professional production (Tahrim-এর মতো)

Runway primary (cinematic) + Kling for hero shots (photorealism) + Pika for stylized effects = complete suite।

2. Social media creator (Reels/TikTok)

Pika primary (fast + creative + free tier) + occasional Kling for special photorealism need।

3. E-commerce product video

Kling primary (photorealistic product). Image-to-video (static photo → motion) particularly strong।

4. Wedding/event videography

Kling for realistic scenes + Runway for cinematic transitions।

5. Educational content / explainer

Pika (stylized illustration) + Runway (text-on-video, editing)।
