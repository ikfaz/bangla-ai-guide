ভূমিকা

Virtual Assistant (VA) — remote administrative support। US/UK busy professional + small business-এর কাছে VA standard hire। বাংলাদেশ থেকে English-fluent VA-দের চাহিদা huge — $500-2500/মাস typical।

VA service categories

  • Executive Assistant: calendar, email, travel — $20-50/hour
  • Social media manager: $300-1500/মাস
  • Content VA: blog, newsletter — $500-2000
  • Customer support VA: $400-1500
  • Real estate VA: listings, leads — $600-2000
  • E-commerce VA: Shopify, Amazon — $500-2000
  • Bookkeeping VA: QuickBooks — $800-3000

AI-augmented tasks

  • Email management: AI sort + draft replies
  • Scheduling: Calendly + AI conflict resolve
  • Travel booking: AI compare + book
  • Research: Perplexity deep dive
  • Data entry: AI OCR + verify
  • Social media post: AI write + schedule
  • Report generation: AI from raw data

Tools stack

  • Communication: Slack, Zoom, Loom
  • Project management: Asana, Trello, Notion
  • Email: Gmail + Boomerang
  • Calendar: Calendly, Google Calendar
  • File share: Google Drive, Dropbox
  • Time tracking: Toggl, Time Doctor
  • Password: 1Password, LastPass

Client acquisition

  • Upwork: primary platform
  • Belay, Time etc: dedicated VA agency
  • OnlineJobs.ph: Philippines competition tough
  • Direct: Twitter founder DM
  • VA-specific Facebook groups: US-based
  • Referral: existing client

Common client industries

  • Real estate agent
  • Online coach/consultant
  • YouTuber/content creator
  • SaaS founder
  • E-commerce store owner
  • Lawyer/professional service
  • Author/speaker

Hourly vs retainer

  • Hourly: $10-30/hour (BD VA range)
  • Retainer: 20-40 hours/মাস at $400-1500
  • Project: Specific deliverable
  • Best: Mix — 1-2 retainer + projects

Time zone management

  • BD is GMT+6
  • US East: GMT-5 (11 hours behind)
  • US West: GMT-8 (14 hours behind)
  • UK: GMT (6 hours behind)
  • Strategy: morning EU client, evening US client
  • Or: dedicated overnight shift for US East

Skills inventory

  • English: fluent essential
  • Time management: client task juggle
  • Reliability: response time
  • Tech-savvy: tool adoption speed
  • Initiative: proactive suggestion
  • Confidentiality: NDA respect

Career progression

  • Year 1: General VA, $500-1500/মাস
  • Year 2: Specialize, $1500-3000
  • Year 3: Niche expert, $3000-6000
  • Year 5+: Agency owner, multiple VAs under you

Payment + tax

  • Upwork → Payoneer → bKash
  • Direct → Wise / PayPal
  • NBR foreign income declare
  • Annual tax filing
  • Save 15-25% for taxes

উপসংহার

VA service — entry-level high-pay remote career। বাংলাদেশ থেকে English ability + AI productivity = win. ৩-৬ মাসে $1500/মাস realistic। আজকেই Upwork-এ VA profile create করুন।
