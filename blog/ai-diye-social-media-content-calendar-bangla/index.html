ভূমিকা

প্রতিদিন social media-তে কী post করব — এই প্রশ্ন creator/business owner-এর সবচেয়ে time-consuming অংশ। AI এই কাজ ৪-৫ ঘণ্টা থেকে ৬০ মিনিটে কমাতে পারে।

AI master prompt — 30-day calendar

  • "Create 30-day social media content calendar for [niche/business]. Audience: [demographic]. Platforms: [FB/IG/TikTok/LinkedIn]. Goal: [awareness/sales/engagement].
    Output table:
    - Day | Platform | Format (post/reel/carousel/story) | Topic | Hook | Caption draft (Bangla) | Hashtags
    Include: 5 educational, 8 engagement, 5 promotional, 5 personal/behind-scene, 4 user-generated, 3 trending. Themes per week."

Calendar structure — proven pattern

  • Monday: Educational/Tip
  • Tuesday: Behind-the-scenes
  • Wednesday: Engagement (poll/question)
  • Thursday: Promotional
  • Friday: User-generated/testimonial
  • Saturday: Entertaining/personality
  • Sunday: Inspirational/recap

Platform-specific adjustments

Facebook

  • Longer caption OK
  • Mixed media: photo + Reel
  • 3-5 post/week
  • Group cross-post
  • Bangla audience focused

Instagram

  • Reels 5+/week
  • Carousel for education
  • Story daily
  • Lifestyle aesthetic
  • Hashtag mix

LinkedIn

  • Long-form post 2-3/week
  • Document/PDF share
  • Thought leadership
  • Industry-specific
  • English primarily

TikTok

  • Daily Reels
  • Trending audio
  • Quick education/entertainment
  • Young audience

Tools to execute calendar

  • Planning: Notion / Google Sheets
  • Scheduling: Buffer, Hootsuite, Meta Business Suite
  • Design: Canva AI bulk template
  • Video: CapCut AI
  • Caption: ChatGPT/Claude
  • Analytics: native platform + Buffer

Weekly batch production

  • Sunday: 7-day calendar AI generate
  • Monday: Visual create (Canva)
  • Tuesday: Reel/video record
  • Wednesday: Edit + caption
  • Thursday: Schedule (Buffer)
  • Friday-Saturday: Engagement only
  • Time/week: 4-6 hours total

Content pillar strategy

  • Pick 3-5 core pillars
  • Example boutique: Style tip, New arrival, Customer story, Behind-scene, Fashion trend
  • Each post must fit a pillar
  • Mix throughout week
  • Audience knows what to expect

Bangladesh-specific events calendar

  • Pohela Boishakh (April 14)
  • Eid (twice yearly)
  • Durga Puja (autumn)
  • Victory Day (Dec 16)
  • Independence Day (Mar 26)
  • International Mother Language Day (Feb 21)
  • Plan campaigns around

Trend-jacking

  • Daily trending hashtag check
  • Cricket match real-time
  • Local news angle
  • Viral video remix (creator only)
  • Holiday-specific
