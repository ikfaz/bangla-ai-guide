Notion AI vs ClickUp AI — Productivity-তে কোন AI ভালো?

ভূমিকা

Productivity platform-এ AI integration এখন standard। Notion ও ClickUp — দুই market leader তাদের AI feature দিয়ে দ্রুত evolve করছে। কোনটি আপনার team-এর জন্য?

Notion AI overview

  • Best for: Documentation, knowledge base, creative writing
  • AI features: Q&A across pages, summarize, write, translate, brainstorm
  • দাম: $১০/user/মাস (add-on); Business plan-এ included

ClickUp AI overview

  • Best for: Project management, task automation
  • AI features: Task generation, status summary, meeting notes, sprint planning
  • দাম: $৭/user/মাস (add-on)

Writing capability

  • Notion AI: Best-in-class — long-form excellent
  • ClickUp AI: Good but task-focused
  • Winner: Notion

Project management

  • Notion: Database flexible কিন্তু not PM-focused
  • ClickUp: Gantt, time tracking, sprint, deep PM
  • Winner: ClickUp by far

AI automation

  • Notion AI: Manual trigger
  • ClickUp AI: Automation rules + AI actions
  • Winner: ClickUp

Knowledge base + search

  • Notion AI: Cross-page search powerful
  • ClickUp AI: Search built-in but doc-feature less
  • Winner: Notion for KB

বাংলাদেশি team-এর সুপারিশ

  • Tech startup: Notion (documentation heavy)
  • Agency (client work): ClickUp (PM heavy)
  • Solo entrepreneur: Notion (cheaper, multi-purpose)
  • Engineering team: ClickUp
  • Content team: Notion

উপসংহার

উভয় platform-এর AI strong, কিন্তু purpose আলাদা। Writing/knowledge-heavy কাজে Notion; PM/task automation-এ ClickUp। কিছু team দুটোই use করে — একটি knowledge base হিসেবে, অন্যটি execution-এ।
