১২টি প্রধান টুল

  1. Remove Background: এক click-এ ক্লিন বের। Saiful-এর primary use।
  2. Cleanup: Unwanted object/person/text remove (Photoshop content-aware fill-এর সমান)।
  3. Image Upscaler: Low-res photo-কে 2x/4x sharp করা। Daraz listing-এ critical।
  4. Relight: Photo-র lighting AI দিয়ে adjust (golden hour, soft light, studio light)।
  5. Remove Text: Watermark, caption, sign remove।
  6. Replace Background: White/branded/abstract background swap।
  7. Image Variations: একটা photo থেকে multiple variation generate।
  8. Reimagine XL: Photo-কে artistic style-এ reimagine।
  9. Sketch to Image: Hand-drawn sketch → photo-realistic image।
  10. Stable Doodle: Doodle + prompt → finished art।
  11. Uncrop: Image-এর border extend (outpainting)। Aspect ratio change কাজে আসে।
  12. Product Photography: Product image-কে professional studio shot-এ transform।

দাম

  • Free: Daily limit-এ 100 image processing। Watermark-ed output।
  • Pro $৭/মাস (annual) / $১৪/মাস (monthly): Unlimited, no watermark, higher resolution, priority।
  • API: Per-call pricing — developers-এর জন্য।
  • BD payment: USD card (Brac/City/EBL Mastercard via bKash recharge)।
