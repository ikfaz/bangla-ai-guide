ভূমিকা

Video editing — content creator economy-এর সবচেয়ে in-demand skill। YouTube + Reels + TikTok explosion। AI দিয়ে editing speed ৫-১০x, beginner-ও professional output produce করতে পারে।

Niche selection

  • YouTube long-form: $50-300/video (10-30 min)
  • YouTube Shorts: $20-100/clip
  • TikTok/Reels: $15-80
  • Podcast clips: $30-100
  • Wedding cinematic: $200-2000
  • Corporate explainer: $300-1500
  • Course content: $50-200

AI-accelerated workflow

  • Auto-cut silence: Descript, CapCut
  • Auto-caption: CapCut Bangla
  • B-roll suggest: AI matches to script
  • Music sync: CapCut beat detection
  • Color grade: AI LUT apply
  • Voice enhance: Adobe Podcast
  • Background remove: Runway
  • Time: 5-min video = 30-60 min edit

Tools stack

  • Free: CapCut (mobile + desktop)
  • Pro: DaVinci Resolve (free pro version)
  • Premium: Adobe Premiere ($23/মাস)
  • Auto-edit: Opus Clip, Descript
  • Caption: Veed.io, Rev
  • Stock: Pexels (free), Storyblocks (paid)

Niche choice — what to specialize

  • YouTuber editing (US/EU client highest pay)
  • Educator (course creators)
  • Coach/consultant (content marketing)
  • Real estate video
  • E-commerce product video
  • Wedding/event
  • Music video

Client acquisition — first 5

  • Upwork: 30-50 application/week
  • YouTube creator DM (small + growing)
  • Fiverr: gigged listing
  • LinkedIn: video specialist headline
  • Facebook BD video editor group
  • Referral from first happy client

Portfolio building

  • Free edit 3 sample videos
  • Before/after demo
  • Niche-specific samples
  • YouTube channel: own showcase
  • Vimeo professional portfolio

Pricing tiers

  • Beginner: $20-50/video
  • Intermediate: $50-150/video
  • Expert: $150-500/video
  • Retainer: $1000-5000/মাস (multiple videos)
  • BD client BDT: ৳1000-5000/video

Client communication

  • Brief template (style, length, deadline)
  • Mid-edit review (avoid full re-do)
  • Revision limit specify
  • Project tracker shared
  • Daily/weekly update

Scaling strategy

  • Solo: 30-50 videos/month max
  • Hire editor: train + delegate
  • Specialize 1 niche dominate
  • Templates create — speed
  • Course/tutorial sell

Income progression (BD freelancer)

  • Month 1-3: $200-1000 (learning client)
  • Month 4-6: $1000-3000
  • Year 1: $3000-8000/মাস
  • Top performer: $10,000+/মাস
