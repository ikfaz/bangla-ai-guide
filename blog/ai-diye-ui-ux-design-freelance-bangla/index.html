Tools stack

  • Primary: Figma (industry standard)
  • AI assistants: Figma AI, Galileo, Magician
  • Prototyping: Figma Prototype, ProtoPie
  • Mockup gen: Mockup AI, Placeit
  • Color/typography: Coolors, Fontjoy
  • Inspiration: Dribbble, Mobbin, Lapa

AI design workflow

  • Wireframe: AI text-to-wireframe (Uizard)
  • Mockup: Galileo AI text-to-design
  • Icons: AI generate (Iconfu)
  • Copy: ChatGPT UX writing
  • User flow: AI suggest screens
  • Variations: design A/B AI generate

Client acquisition

  • Upwork: 30-50/week applications first
  • Dribbble Pro: client outreach
  • LinkedIn: SaaS founder DM
  • BD startups: Pathao, Chaldal alumni
  • YC startups: design rate higher
  • Referrals: existing client request

Design process

  • Day 1-2: Client brief + research
  • Day 3-4: Wireframe (AI assist)
  • Day 5-7: Visual design
  • Day 8-9: Prototype + review
  • Day 10: Hand-off + documentation
  • Typical: $1500-3000 for 10-day project

Skills beyond Figma

  • User research: interview, survey
  • Information architecture: nav structure
  • Interaction design: micro-interactions
  • Motion design: Principle, Lottie
  • Front-end basics: talk dev language
  • Business strategy: design for outcome

Common ভুল

  • Pretty over usable
  • Trending design vs context fit
  • No user research
  • Skip wireframe → straight visual
  • No design rationale (when client asks)
  • Single style portfolio (not versatile)

Income progression

  • Month 1-3: $300-1500 (first clients)
  • Month 4-6: $1500-4000
  • Year 1: $3000-10000/মাস
  • Senior: $8000-30000/মাস
