Rumana-র 6-step AI Workflow

Step 1: Donor Research (Perplexity Pro, 30 min)

"নিচের donor — Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation। আমি BD-based NGO (Microfinance + WASH + Education focus)। বলো:
(১) Their 2026 priority area + funding allocation,
(২) Past 5 years funded project in Bangladesh,
(৩) Application timeline + review cycle,
(৪) Key program officer + their background,
(৫) Common rejection reasons + writing pitfall to avoid।"

Step 2: Concept Note Drafting (Claude, 2 hours)

5-page concept note (project overview, target population, intervention logic, expected outcome, budget summary):

"নিচের project idea: BD-এর হাওর region (Sunamganj district)-এ climate-resilient agriculture — flood-tolerant rice variety + diversified livelihood (poultry + fish farming)। Target: 5,000 household over 3 years।

Concept note draft করো donor-language (technical, evidence-driven, theory-of-change framework, SDG alignment, measurable indicator)। 5-page structured।"

Step 3: Full Proposal Expansion (Claude Pro Project, 3-5 days)

  • Concept note approved → full proposal (40-80 pages)।
  • Sections: Executive Summary, Problem Statement, Project Description, Theory of Change, Logframe, Budget, Implementation Plan, M&E, Sustainability, Risk Matrix।
  • Claude-এ Project-এ all past proposal + donor previous winners + sector benchmark upload।
  • Section-by-section drafting + iteration।

Step 4: Budget Development (Excel + Claude, 1 day)

"$1.5M, 3-year project budget breakdown for haor climate-resilient agriculture। Categories: Personnel (40%), Programs/Activities (35%), Equipment (10%), M&E (5%), Admin (10%)। Donor-compliant formatting। Detailed line item with cost justification।"

Step 5: Compliance Check (Manual + AI)

  • Donor-specific format requirement।
  • Mandatory annex (organizational profile, audit, board resolution, CV)।
  • Cross-cutting issue (gender, environment, disability) inclusion।
  • Word/page limit strict adherence।

Step 6: Submission + Follow-up (1 hour)

  • Electronic submission portal upload।
  • Acknowledgment email।
  • ২ week follow-up if no response।
  • Internal database update — track status।

4 Critical Mistakes — 60% Proposals Reject Reasons

  • 1. Generic language: "We will empower communities" — non-specific। Donor wants measurable outcome।
  • 2. Weak logframe: Objective → Activity → Output → Outcome chain — illogical। Theory of change must be tight।
  • 3. Budget-narrative misalignment: Budget $500K, narrative says $1.5M activity scope। Reviewer instantly catches।
  • 4. Compliance miss: Donor-mandated section skipped (gender mainstreaming, risk matrix, sustainability)। Auto-reject।

BD-Specific Donor Strategy

  • USAID/PACT: SAM registration + indirect cost rate । Compliance-heavy।
  • FCDO (UK): Value-for-money emphasis + outcome-based budget।
  • JICA: Japan-tied procurement + technical assistance bundling।
  • Bill & Melinda Gates: Innovation + evidence-based scale পথ।
  • Local government partnership: Bangladesh PSO + DPHE collaborations boost competitiveness।
