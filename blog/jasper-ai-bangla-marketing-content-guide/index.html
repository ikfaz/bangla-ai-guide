ভূমিকা — Banani-র agency-র Jasper investment

বনানীর "Onushilon Media"-র কথা আগের গাইডে এসেছিল (jasper-vs-copy-vs-writesonic ব্যাচে)। Content Director নাইমা চৌধুরী সেখানে Copy.ai পছন্দ করেছিলেন — workflow + CRM-এর জন্য। কিন্তু তাঁর agency-র parent company-র US client "Veritas Healthcare BD" (Apollo Hospital-এর US-affiliated brand) এ Jasper required — strict brand voice + HIPAA-aware content + multi-language localization। ২০২৫-এ Naima-র team-এর জন্য একটা dedicated Jasper Business plan ($৬৯/user/মাস × ৪ writers = $২৭৬/মাস)। ৬ মাস পর: Veritas client retainer ৳৩.৫ লাখ → ৳৫.৮ লাখ/মাস, brand voice consistency 98%, multi-language output (Bangla/English/Hindi) seamless। এই গাইড সেই enterprise-grade workflow।

এই গাইডে যা থাকবে: Jasper কী, ChatGPT-এর সাথে honest পার্থক্য, দাম (enterprise reality), প্রথম campaign workflow, কখন Jasper, কখন overkill, BD use cases (যেখানে justified)।

দাম

  • Creator $৪৯/user/মাস: 1 brand voice, basic templates। Solo professional।
  • Pro $৬৯/user/মাস: Naima-র team-এর tier। Multi-brand voice (3 brands), Surfer SEO integration, campaign tools।
  • Business $২৪৯+/user/মাস: Unlimited brand voices, custom workflows, API, dedicated CSM।
  • Annual discount: ~17-20% off।
  • BD payment: USD card via bKash, Stripe। Agency-এ usually corporate card।

প্রথম campaign workflow — Naima-র Veritas Healthcare

Campaign brief: "Heart Health Awareness Month — Bangladesh + US dual market"
  1. Brand Voice training (one-time):
    • Veritas-এর past 20 article + brand guideline upload
    • Voice attributes: empathetic, science-backed, BD/US cultural sensitivity
    • Jasper learns + creates persistent voice profile
  2. Campaign setup:
    • 20-article series outline
    • Bangla + English version each
    • Distribution channels: blog, FB, LinkedIn, email, YouTube script
  3. Per-article workflow:
    • Topic + audience + tone select
    • Brand Voice "Veritas Healthcare" select
    • Generate (Bangla version first)
    • Review + edit (subject matter expert physician verifies medical claim)
    • English version generate from Bangla outline
    • Surfer SEO optimization integration
    • Publish
  4. Multi-channel adapt:
    • Article → FB caption (Jasper Template)
    • Article → LinkedIn long-form post
    • Article → email newsletter version
    • Article → 60-second YouTube script
  5. Output: 20 articles × 4 formats = 80 pieces in 4 weeks (4 writers × Jasper)। আগে ছিল 60-pieces/8 weeks-এর pace।

কখন Jasper বেছে নেবেন?

  • 5+ writer team in agency/enterprise
  • Multi-brand portfolio (3+ clients with distinct voices)
  • Volume content (50+ pieces/মাস)
  • SEO-driven content strategy (Surfer SEO integration valuable)
  • Compliance-aware industries (healthcare, finance, legal)
  • Multi-language requirement (Bangla + English + Hindi + Arabic, etc.)
  • Budget allowed ($২৫০+/মাস team tier)

কখন overkill

  • Solo freelancer (Rytr/ChatGPT Plus sufficient)
  • Single-brand small business
  • Content volume <10 pieces/মাস
  • Budget <$৫০/মাস
  • Bangla-heavy কাজ (ChatGPT Plus better quality)
