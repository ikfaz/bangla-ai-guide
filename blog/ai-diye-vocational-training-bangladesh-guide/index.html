ভূমিকা

TVET (Technical and Vocational Education and Training) — বাংলাদেশের workforce-এর foundation। Polytechnic, vocational school, NGO program। AI এই training-কে modernize, accessible করতে পারে।

TVET landscape — Bangladesh

  • BTEB (Bangladesh Technical Education Board) regulated
  • 60+ polytechnic
  • 4,500+ TVET institute
  • 5 lakh+ student annually
  • Skills: electrical, plumbing, IT, automotive, garments, food, etc.
