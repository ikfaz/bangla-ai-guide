Workflow উদাহরণ — Nusrat-এর Upwork project

Client: US SaaS company, blog content (1500-word, weekly)
  1. Topic + brief Slack-এ receive (Sokal 8 AM BD time)
  2. Rytr-এ "Blog Post" template → topic title + keywords paste
  3. AI generates outline → Nusrat review + adjust (5 min)
  4. Section-by-section expand → AI draft
  5. Manual heavy edit: BD context examples add, factual claims verify, brand voice match (30-45 min)
  6. Grammar pass (Rytr built-in)
  7. SEO optimization: meta description, title tag — Rytr template
  8. WordPress upload + image
  9. Delivery: 2-3 hours total (vs 5-6 hours manual writing)

Per blog rate $৪০। Daily 2 blogs × 5 days = $৪০০/সপ্তাহ। Monthly $১,৬০০ achievable (current $৬২০ at fewer hours)।
