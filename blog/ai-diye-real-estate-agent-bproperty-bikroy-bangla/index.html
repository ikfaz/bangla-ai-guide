প্রকাশিত: ২৬ মে, ২০২৬

ভূমিকা — Banani-র এক real estate agent-এর pivot

বনানীর ১১ নং রোডের real estate agent শাহেদ আহমেদ — ৩৭ বছর, BBA NSU ২০১০, ১২ বছর Bproperty.com-এ B2C Sales Manager। ২০২৪ সেপ্টেম্বরে independent agent হলেন — Banani/Gulshan/Baridhara high-end residential focus। প্রথম ৪ মাসে struggle: monthly 1-2 deals, commission income ৳৪০-৬০K (Bproperty-তে ছিল ৳১.৪L stable salary)। Listing limited, lead conversion poor। ২০২৫ ফেব্রুয়ারিতে AI workflow integrate। ১৪ মাস পর: monthly 5-8 deals, commission ৳৩.২-৪.৮ লাখ, listing 8 → 32 active, NRI client (US/UK Bangladeshi expat) ৪০% portfolio। এই গাইড সেই full workflow।

এই গাইডে যা থাকবে: Listing description AI workflow, photo enhancement (DIY → professional), virtual tour + 3D for NRI buyers, lead nurture (WhatsApp + chatbot automation), market analysis, marketing content calendar, legal document support, tools stack।

১. Listing description AI workflow

আগের article-এ (real-estate-bproperty-listing — Imran-এর গল্প) আমরা Bashundhara case দেখেছিলাম। Shahed-এর Banani high-end focus আলাদা পরিস্থিতি:

  • ৫-৬ কোটি+ apartment buyer profile different — professional, NRI, ultra-high-net-worth
  • Description-এ premium language + lifestyle narrative critical
  • Per listing AI workflow: ChatGPT-এ "Banani এই 4500 sqft penthouse-এর descriptions লেখো — luxury market positioning, US/UK return-আসা NRI বুঝবে। English + Bangla version।"
  • Output: 250-word English + 200-word Bangla, lifestyle focus (rooftop terrace, modern security, school proximity), price point appropriate

২. Photo enhancement

ClipDrop + Snapseed combo (আগের article-এ Saiful-এর Daraz workflow-এ describe-করা)। Shahed-এর extra:

  • Drone footage drone owner থেকে commission rent ৳২,০০০/property (vs ৳২৫,০০০ agency)
  • Virtual staging (empty room → furnished AI) — RoomGPT/Apply Design
  • HDR + wide-angle phone photography
  • Per listing photo cost ৳৩,০০০ → ৳৩৫০

৩. Virtual tour + 3D

Shahed-এর NRI client 40% — virtual tour critical:

  • Smartphone walkthrough video (4-5 min) → Matterport-like AI tool → 3D dollhouse
  • Listing-এ embedded 360° tour
  • WhatsApp video call live walkthrough + Q&A
  • NRI buyer conversion: in-person visit লাগে না — full virtual close

৪. Lead nurture (WhatsApp + chatbot)

Shahed-এর automation:
  • Bproperty/Bikroy inquiry → AI categorize (serious/shopping/NRI/time-waster)
  • Serious buyer: immediate WhatsApp + 2 alternative options + booking link
  • NRI: scheduled video call + virtual tour link
  • Drip nurture: Day 3, 7, 14, 30 — new listings matching criteria
  • Old inquiries (60+ days inactive): re-engagement campaign — "Banani-এ price down 5% — interested?"
  • Lead conversion 4% → 22%

৫. Market analysis

  • "Banani Block H per sqft price trend last 36 months — analyze + predict next 12 months direction" — ChatGPT data crunch
  • Hot zones: "Dhaka-তে next 2 years-এ যেসব area-তে price ১৫-২৫% appreciate করবে — top 8 + reasoning" — AI-generated investment thesis
  • NRI-targeted: "Foreign Direct Investment via real estate — BD recent policy + tax implications" — comprehensive briefing
  • Competitor analysis: Bproperty + Bikroy + ShareTrip Property listings comparison

৬. Marketing — content calendar

  • FB page: Daily AI-generated property tip + market insight
  • Instagram: Listing reels (CapCut + Auto Captions)
  • LinkedIn: Weekly long-form market analysis post — establish thought leadership
  • YouTube channel: Monthly market update video (D-ID talking avatar use)
  • Newsletter: Monthly market report PDF (AI-generated insights + own commentary) — premium NRI clients

৭. Legal document support

  • Sale deed initial review: "এই sale deed-এর 8 pages-এ 3টা potential issue identify করো"
  • Mutation process Bangla guide for NRI buyers
  • RAJUK approval verify checklist
  • Joint-venture agreement template review
  • Tax implications (capital gains, registration fee, stamp duty)
  • ⚠️ Final legal advice always from lawyer। AI primary research only।

  • ChatGPT Plus $২০ — primary AI
  • ClipDrop $৭/মাস — photo enhancement
  • Matterport-like (Asteroom) $৯/মাস — 3D tours
