২০২৬-এ Image-to-GIF AI Tool Stack

  • Pika Labs (image-to-loop GIF): Best for product spin + lifestyle motion। $৮/মাস starter।
  • Kling AI: Asian-context strong, 6-second motion → GIF export।
  • LottieFiles + AI: Vector-based animated GIF, lightweight for web।
  • Adobe Photoshop AI Fill + Timeline: Frame-by-frame professional control।
  • Canva + Magic Animate: Beginner-friendly, template-rich।
  • Runway Gen-3 image-to-video → GIF: Cinematic quality।
  • Ezgif.com (free): Final compression + optimization।

Ritika-র Per-Asset Workflow

Step 1: Concept (2 min)

  • Product photo (still)।
  • Goal: Showcase one specific feature (color shift, application motion, packaging reveal)।
  • Output spec: 9:16 vertical (Reel) বা 1:1 square (feed)।

Step 2: AI animation (3 min)

Pika Labs prompt: "Slow rotating spin of lipstick tube, soft golden lighting, white seamless background, glossy reflection on tube cap, 3-second smooth loop"।

Step 3: Export + optimize (1 min)

  • MP4 → Ezgif converts to GIF।
  • File size optimize <2MB (Facebook auto-compress otherwise)।
  • Loop seamless।

4 BD Use Cases

  • D2C product showcase: Lipstick spin, saree drape, food sizzle। Ritika-র primary।
  • E-learning explainer: Short concept loop (math formula, science process)। 10MS-style use।
  • Marketing email: Animated GIF in email = 26% higher CTR vs static।
  • WhatsApp marketing broadcast: Eye-catching GIF for promo announcement।

3 Optimization Tips

  • 1. Loop seamless: First + last frame identical = no jarring restart।
  • 2. File size <2MB: Facebook compress otherwise = quality loss।
  • 3. Mobile-first design: Most BD viewing phone। 9:16 vertical winning।
