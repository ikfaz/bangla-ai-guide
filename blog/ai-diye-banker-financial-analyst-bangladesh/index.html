BD Banking/Finance Industry 2026

  • Workforce: ৩.৪ লাখ banking professional, ১.১ লাখ in private banks।

৩টা Critical Data-Leak Risk

সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ section:
  • Customer name/account/NID: Never paste। Always anonymize (Customer-A, Account-1)।
  • Bank-internal pricing/strategy: Confidential — leaked AI training data via OpenAI/Anthropic possible। Use anonymous abstractions।
  • Trade secret algorithm: Bank's credit scoring formula, fraud detection rule — don't paste। AI competitor learning risk।
  • Compliance violation potential: Bangladesh Bank-এর Information Security Guideline (2023) — personally identifiable data third-party AI service-এ leak punishable। Internal AI Tool (e.g., on-premise GPT) for sensitive task।

BD Banking AI Tool Stack

  • ChatGPT Plus / Claude Pro: $20/mo. Anonymized analysis OK।
  • Perplexity Pro: Industry research, citation-grounded।
  • Excel + Copilot: Microsoft 365 enterprise license।
  • Bloomberg Terminal AI: $25K/year — enterprise।
  • Refinitiv Eikon AI: Capital market specific।
  • Internal AI (Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock): On-premise/private — data secure।
