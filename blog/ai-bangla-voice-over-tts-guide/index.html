Sajeda-র Voice Cloning Workflow (One-time setup)

Step 1: Voice sample recording (1 hour)

  • Professional studio (or quiet home + good mic ৳৪,০০০ Audio-Technica AT2020)।
  • 30-45 min Bangla reading: diverse content (news script, story, instructional, emotional)।
  • 15 min English code-switching dialogue।
  • WAV 44.1kHz format, no background noise।

Step 2: ElevenLabs Voice Lab upload

  • Voice Lab → "Create Voice" → "Instant Voice Clone"।
  • Upload ৩-৫ min sample (best quality)।
  • Name + description।
  • Within 30 seconds — your AI voice ready।

Step 3: Test + refine

  • Test text-to-speech with various tone (news, emotional, ad)।
  • If unnatural — re-record sample + upgrade to "Professional Voice Clone" ($১৯ one-time, requires 30+ min sample)।
  • "Professional" version 95%+ matches your voice।
