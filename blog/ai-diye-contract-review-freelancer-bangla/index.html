২০২৬-এ Freelance Contract Reality

  • Upwork/Fiverr-এর built-in contract: Standard, low-risk। Custom client-supplied বেশি risky।
  • US/EU/UK client-এর custom NDA + MSA + SOW: 15-50 pages, dense legal language।
  • Common pain: IP transfer, unlimited revision, payment terms, jurisdiction, non-compete।
  • BD freelancer naive: "Looks fine" sign — later expensive lesson।

৭টা Critical Red Flag

  1. IP transfer pre-payment: "Deliverables transfer upon project start" — withhold leverage zero।
  2. Unlimited revisions: "Until client satisfied" — endless scope creep।
  3. Payment terms vague: "Net-30 from approval" — approval delay = payment delay forever।
  4. Jurisdiction unfavorable: "California courts" — BD-based freelancer pursue impossible।
  5. Non-compete broad: "12 months, all industries" — career-limiting।
  6. Indemnification one-sided: "Freelancer indemnifies client for all damages" — unlimited liability।
  7. Termination penalty: "Freelancer pays 50% project value if terminates early" — exit-hostile।

Tasnia-র AI Workflow (per Contract)

Step 1: Full contract paste + structural analysis (Claude, 5 min)

"নিচের ১২-page MSA (Master Service Agreement) attached। আমি BD-based freelance UX designer। Client US Delaware LLC।

Analyze:
(১) Clause-by-clause summary,
(২) Top 5 red flag clause identification,
(৩) Negotiation talking point for each red flag,
(৪) Industry-standard benchmark comparison,
(৫) Overall risk rating: low/medium/high।"

Step 2: Specific clause deep-dive (10 min)

Red flag-গুলোর প্রতিটায় follow-up: "এই clause-এর alternate wording yang freelancer-friendly: present 3 options progressive (mild/moderate/aggressive) negotiation।"

Step 3: Comparison with industry standard (5 min)

"নিচের clause: [paste]। 2026 US freelance industry standard MSA-এ এই type-এর clause কী wording থাকে? আমার current vs standard — explain difference।"

Step 4: Counter-proposal drafting (10 min)

"আমার freelancer-friendly version of this contract draft করো — same business intent preserved, কিন্তু IP transfer post-payment, defined scope, BD-jurisdiction option, mutual indemnification।"

When to Hire a Real Lawyer

  • $10K+ project value: Lawyer fee $500-1,500 — affordable insurance।
  • Multi-year retainer: Long-term exposure।
  • Equity/Revenue share: Complex IP + tax implication।
  • International cross-border: Multiple jurisdiction।
  • NDA-violation accusation: Past contract dispute।

For these scenarios — AI helps prep questions, but lawyer-final-review mandatory।

BD-Context AI Tool Stack

  • Claude Pro ($20/mo): Best for long contract analysis।
  • ChatGPT Plus: Alternative + good for quick query।
  • Lawyer (BD): Barrister Mahmudul-style — $৭০-১৫০ per contract review।
  • HelloSign/DocuSign: Final signed contract storage।
