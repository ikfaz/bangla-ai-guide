প্রকাশিত: ২৬ মে, ২০২৬

ভূমিকা

বাংলাদেশের আইন পেশায় AI integration ধীরগতিতে শুরু হয়েছে। দ্রুত adapter-রা সবচেয়ে বেশি সুবিধা পাবেন — research time ৭০% কমে, document drafting দ্বিগুণ দ্রুত হয়, এবং junior lawyer-এর productivity বহুগুণ বাড়ে।

১. Legal research

  • ChatGPT/Claude: Case law analysis, principle extraction
  • Westlaw, LexisNexis AI: Premium specialized (expensive)
  • Perplexity: Bangladesh Supreme Court verdicts search
  • Manupatra: South Asian legal database
  • Approach: AI starts research, lawyer verifies primary source

২. Contract drafting

  • ChatGPT prompt: "Draft a sale deed for residential plot, Dhaka, between [parties], including standard warranties"
  • Claude long-context: Long agreement review
  • Spellbook (Word add-in): Contract automation
  • Iterate: First draft, then specific clause refine

৩. Contract review

  • Upload contract → "Flag risky clauses, suggest negotiation points"
  • Compare with standard template: "Which clauses differ from standard NDA?"
  • Plain-language summary: Client-এর জন্য Bangla-তে

৪. Document automation

  • Affidavit, plaint, written statement template: AI assist
  • Bulk document (eg, 50 notice): Mail merge + AI personalization
  • Court filing checklist: AI generates

৫. Client communication

  • Bangla legal explanation: "Explain section 138 NI Act in simple Bangla to a businessman"
  • Demand notice drafting: Quick first draft
  • Email follow-up: Tone professional
  • Status update template: Recurring

৬. Case management

  • Notion/ClickUp + AI for case tracker
  • Deadline reminder
  • Document version control
  • Time tracking → invoice

৭. Junior lawyer training

  • AI Socratic dialogue — "Quiz me on Contract Act sections"
  • Mock argument: AI as opposing counsel
  • Pleading practice with feedback
  • Bangladesh legal database self-study

সতর্কতা

  • Confidentiality: Client identifier AI-তে না
  • Hallucination: AI fake case cite করতে পারে — verify
  • Bangladesh-specific law: AI training-এ limited; primary source check
  • Bar Council rule: AI use disclosure কোন context-এ
  • Professional liability: AI suggestion lawyer's responsibility

বাংলাদেশি law firm-এর জন্য roadmap

  • Phase 1: ChatGPT Plus for partners (legal research speed)
  • Phase 2: Document automation template build
  • Phase 3: Client management system + AI
  • Phase 4: Specialty: M&A, IP — premium AI invest

উপসংহার

AI আইনজীবীর কাজের ৩০-৫০% repetitive task automate করতে পারে — যাতে আপনি client interaction ও strategic thinking-এ বেশি সময় দিতে পারেন। আজই ChatGPT-তে একটা contract review করে দেখুন।
