Mahmudul-এর 6 AI Use Cases

1. Case Law Research

"Bangladesh-এর Penal Code 1860, Section 420 (Cheating) — last 15 years-এর Supreme Court precedent। Key holding, ratio decidendi, dissenting opinion। Citation format Bangladesh Law Decisions (BLD)-style।

Specifically: 'Cheating in cyber transaction' relevant case।"

Caveat: AI sometimes hallucinates Bangladesh case citations। Always verify against Supreme Court online repository + Bangladesh Law Reports (BLR)। AI provides 70% time-saving structure, manual verification 100% required।

2. Contract Drafting

"নিচের client brief: BD garments exporter আর European buyer-এর master sales agreement। Volume 50,000 USD/quarter। Payment LC at sight। Delivery terms CIF Chittagong। Force majeure clause দরকার। 3-year tenure।

Draft a master agreement: standard clauses + BD-specific (NBR tax, BG regulation), force majeure with COVID precedent, dispute resolution ICA Dhaka, governing law Bangladesh।"

3. Legal Notice/Demand Letter

Client-এর dispute description → AI-drafted professional legal notice in Bangla/English। Mahmudul edits 30% — strategic specificity + tone।

4. Discovery/Document Review

Litigation discovery-এ 300-page document। Claude-এ paste: "এই documents-এ key admission, contradiction, date-anomaly identify। Privileged/work-product material flag। Timeline reconstruction।"

5. Client Brief Memorandum

"নিচের 90-min client interview transcript থেকে structured client brief বানাও: facts, legal issues, applicable law, preliminary assessment, recommended action with timeline + cost estimate।"

6. Court Filing Preparation

  • Plaint drafting (civil)।
  • Written statement (defendant side)।
  • Affidavit format।
  • Motion drafting।
  • Bail petition with case law support।

BD-Specific Legal AI Workflow

  • Supreme Court Online (free): https://www.supremecourt.gov.bd। Recent judgment search।
  • BLD/BLR Database: Bangladesh Law Reports — paid, premium। ICAB members discount।
  • Manupatra/SCC Online: Indian + Bangladesh case law।
  • ChatGPT/Claude: Generic legal reasoning + drafting।
  • Lexis Nexis / Westlaw (international): Global precedent।
  • Harvey AI (specialized legal AI): Currently enterprise-only।

৪টা Ethical Boundary — Critical for Lawyer

  • 1. Client confidentiality: Bar Council Rule 19, Bangladesh Bar Council Rules। AI service-এ paste = third-party disclosure। Always anonymize identifier।
  • 2. AI hallucinated citations: ২০২৩-এ US lawyer-এর Mata v. Avianca case — AI fabricated citation, sanctioned। Every AI-cited case manually verify mandatory।
  • 3. Practicing law unauthorized: AI cannot give "legal advice" to public। Lawyer responsibility-এর সাথে AI output sign-off required।
  • 4. Privileged communication: Solicitor-client privilege strict in BD। On-premise AI for sensitive cases preferred।

উপসংহার — আপনার আজকের পদক্ষেপ

Mahmudul বলেন: "AI আমার law degree replace করেনি — AI আমার junior associate-এর time-burden কমিয়েছে। আমি এখন ৩৪ client manage করতে পারি আমার আগের 5-attorney firm-এর সমান output-এ। কিন্তু confidentiality protocol — সেটাই difference responsible-vs-reckless lawyer-এর।" আজই করুন: যদি আপনি junior lawyer/legal researcher — Claude.ai-তে যান, একটা past case-এর anonymized fact pattern paste করুন, "এই facts-এর basis-এ Bangladesh applicable law + precedent suggest" prompt। AI 70% structure দেবে, আপনার 30% verification + strategy। ২ ঘণ্টার work ৪০ মিনিটে।
