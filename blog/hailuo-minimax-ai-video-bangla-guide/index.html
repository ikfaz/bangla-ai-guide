Access (BD থেকে)

  1. Website: hailuoai.com/video → Sign up Google/email
  2. VPN: সাধারণত লাগে না (যদিও Chinese platform), BD থেকে accessible
  3. Account: Free tier — daily 10-15 generations (varies)
  4. Mobile app: Android (APK direct + Google Play কিছু কান্ট্রিতে), iOS (App Store)

Basic usage

Text-to-video

  1. Login → "Create Video" → Text input
  2. Prompt: "A traditional Bengali grandmother kneading dough for handmade roti, golden afternoon light, warm kitchen, close-up of weathered hands, photorealistic, slow motion"
  3. Settings: aspect ratio (9:16 / 16:9), duration (6 sec default)
  4. Generate → 2-3 min wait → preview → download (MP4, 720p free / 1080p Pro)

Image-to-video

  1. Upload static image (food photo, product, person)
  2. Motion prompt: "Steam rising from the hot biryani, gentle camera dolly-in"
  3. Generate → motion applied to your image

Limitations

  • Free tier daily limit (10-15 generations) — heavy day-এ exhausted
  • Max 6-second clip (paid tier 10 sec)
  • Resolution 720p free (1080p paid)
  • Watermark on free output (Pro removes)
  • Bangla prompt OK, but English clearer results
  • Complex scene-এ artifact (hand/face deformation)
  • No fine-grained motion control (e.g., specific camera path)

When Hailuo, when paid

Hailuo যথেষ্ট:
  • Social media b-roll (food prep, lifestyle, abstract)
  • Ad teaser (15-second Reel/TikTok)
  • Image-to-video animation (product photo → motion)
  • Concept exploration / mood board
  • Budget-constrained creators
  • Experimentation phase
Paid alternative দরকার:
  • Client deliverable (premium quality required)
  • Long-form video (30+ second sequence)
  • Complex scene with specific narrative
  • Brand identity match (specific style, character consistency)
  • 4K output for big screens / broadcast

Bangla prompt tips

  • Bangla prompt acceptable, but English clearer results (model English-trained-dominant)
  • "Bengali grandmother, sari with red border, traditional kitchen" — cultural context describe (very specific helps)
  • BD-specific: "Dhaka rickshaw at sunset golden hour, monsoon clouds" — works
  • Food: "Steaming hot kacchi biryani, traditional brass plate" — strong
  • Avoid: very complex multi-character scenes (artifact risk)

Use cases (Fahim's actual portfolio)

  1. Restaurant promo (Sylhet-based): Food close-up b-roll → Reels
  2. Fashion product video: Static product photo → 360° rotation effect
  3. Real estate: Property still → ambient motion (curtain sway, plant breeze)
  4. NGO awareness campaign: Conceptual scene (children studying, hands working)
  5. Music video aesthetic shots: Local musician's promo
