4টা Disability Category — AI Tools Map

1. Mobility (motor impairment)

  • Voice-controlled computer: Google Voice Access (Android), Voice Control (iOS, macOS), Windows Voice Access।
  • Voice-to-text: Google Bangla Voice Input, ChatGPT Voice mode।
  • Eye tracking: Tobii (paid) + free webcam-based gaze tracking।
  • One-handed keyboard (Microsoft SwiftKey): Touch typing optimized।

2. Visual (blind / low-vision)

  • Screen reader: NVDA (free Windows) + Bangla TTS, JAWS (paid), VoiceOver (iOS/macOS), TalkBack (Android)।
  • Be My Eyes + Be My AI: Real-time photo description, BD Bangla supported।
  • Seeing AI (Microsoft, free): Camera-pointed object/text description।
  • Magnifier + high contrast: Built-in OS feature।

3. Hearing (deaf / hard-of-hearing)

  • Live caption (Android/iOS): Real-time speech-to-text during call/video।
  • YouTube auto-caption Bangla: 90%+ accuracy।
  • Otter.ai real-time: Meeting transcription।
  • Bangla sign language video resource: Growing collection।

4. Cognitive (learning disability, ADHD, autism)

  • Read Aloud (Edge browser, free): Web content Bangla TTS।
  • Grammarly + ChatGPT: Writing assist।
  • Focus mode (Forest app, FocusMate): ADHD-friendly productivity।
  • NotebookLM: Audio overview for auditory learners।

Tahsin-এর Daily Setup

  • Laptop: MacBook Air M2 (Voice Control native)।
  • Headset mic: ৳৮,০০০ noise-canceling।
  • Class: Otter.ai live transcription, Tahsin reads-along।
  • Notes: Voice-dictate to Apple Notes/Google Docs।
  • Assignment: ChatGPT Voice mode + Claude — discuss verbally, AI types।
  • Communication: WhatsApp Voice messages preferred over typing।

BD Government + NGO Schemes

  • Disability Allowance: Department of Social Services — monthly ৳৮৫০ stipend।
  • Education stipend: Disabled student-এর জন্য special stipend।
  • BRAC Disability Program: Free tool + training।
  • Bangladesh National Federation of the Deaf (BNFD): Resource + advocacy।
  • YPSA (Young Power in Social Action): AI accessibility training।

উপসংহার — আপনার আজকের পদক্ষেপ

Tahsin বলেন: "Disability-র সাথে life-এ থাকা মানে life-এ কম কিছু পাওয়া না — মানে different tool-এ access করা। AI সেই tool-গুলো এখন free + powerful বানিয়েছে। আমি যা করতে পারি — তাঁদের সাহায্যে — সেটা ১৬ লাখ BD-র disabled person-এর জন্য massive opportunity।" আজই করুন: যদি আপনি বা পরিবারের কেউ disabled — উপরের 4 category থেকে relevant tool select করুন। Most are free। Google Voice Access install করুন (Android), বা Seeing AI (visually impaired)। ১৫-মিনিট-এর setup আপনার daily life-এ profound change আনবে।
