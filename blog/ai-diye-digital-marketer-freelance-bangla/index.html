দাও:

  • Heatmap data (Hotjar) → ChatGPT analyze বটটলneck।
  • Existing copy → Claude rewrite ৩টা variation।
  • A/B test setup (Google Optimize / VWO)।
  • ৭ দিনে winner declare।

Pillar 4: Email sequence automation

"Client: B2B SaaS trial-to-paid conversion। Average trial-to-paid currently 8%। ৭-email sequence design করো yang ১০-১২%-এ push করার জন্য। প্রতি email-এ: send timing (day post-trial-start), subject line (3 variant), 200-word body, single CTA, personalization tokens।"

BD-Specific Reality

  • Payment: Wise (best FX), Payoneer (most universal), bank wire (slow but works)। BD-government remittance bonus 2.5%।
  • NBR tax: $৩০K+ annual income → TIN registration। Software/IT export-এ ০% income tax (২০২৭ পর্যন্ত)। CA hire mandatory at $৩K+/মাস।
  • Time zone: Bangladesh +6 GMT। US East -5 = 11-hour difference। UK 0 = 6-hour। Morning + evening overlap pattern।
  • Client trust signals: US LLC formation (Stripe Atlas $৫০০ one-time) — তাঁর agency-as-US-business presentation enable। ৪০% rate uplift।

৪টা mistakes — ৯০% BD digital marketer-কে $৫০০ ceiling-এ আটকায়

  • 1. "I do everything" positioning: Generic profile, low rate compete with India/Philippines। Niche specialization 4x rate।
  • 2. Per-hour pricing: Hour-rate caps your income। Retainer + value-based pricing scaling।
  • 3. Service delivery কারিগর-level: Manual ad management, manual reporting। AI automate দিয়ে service quality higher, time লাগে কম।
  • 4. No personal brand: Cold proposal-only → race-to-bottom। Personal brand → inbound + ৪x rate।
