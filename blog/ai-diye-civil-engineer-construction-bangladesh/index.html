ইমরানের 5 Key AI Use Cases

1. BOQ (Bill of Quantities) generation

"নিচের 6-story residential apartment-এর architectural drawing-এর কী-information attached করছি (square footage, room count, structural elements):
- Ground floor: 2,400 sqft, parking + utility
- Floors 1-5: 1,800 sqft each, 3-bed apartment
- Roof: 800 sqft (water tank + utility)
- Location: Mirpur DOHS

Generate detailed BOQ: Excavation, RCC work, brick masonry, plaster, flooring, electrical, plumbing, finishing। প্রতি item-এ: quantity, unit rate (Mirpur 2026 BD-market), total। Sum + 10% contingency।"

Result: ৩-৪ ঘণ্টার manual কাজ ২০ মিনিটে। ইমরান rate verification নিজে করেন (RAJUK + market visit)।

2. Drawing analysis + clash detection

  • AutoCAD/Revit drawing → Claude Vision upload।
  • "এই structural drawing-এ obvious clash detection বলো: beam-column conflict, MEP routing issue, accessibility violation, BNBC 2020 compliance।"
  • Manual verification, কিন্তু AI scanning ৭০% issue pre-flag।

3. Site report writing (daily)

Site supervisor voice memo + photo → ChatGPT-এ paste → professional daily progress report draft। ৪৫-min-এর কাজ ১০ minute-এ।

4. Vendor negotiation prep

Supplier quotation comparison। AI-এ: "এই ৩টা cement supplier-এর quote: brand, price/bag, payment term, delivery time। For 6-story apartment project, recommend optimal + negotiation talking points (current market price benchmark, alternative supplier leverage)।"

5. RAJUK approval document drafting

  • Application form auto-fill।
  • NOC, structural drawing checklist।
  • Setback compliance verification।
  • Fire safety compliance documentation।

৪টা Mistake — যা ৭০% Civil Engineer-কে stuck রাখে

  • 1. AI BOQ unverified submit: AI numbers ৮৫% accurate, ১৫% off। Site visit + supplier check mandatory।
  • 2. Code compliance AI-blind trust: BNBC + DAP regulation frequently update। AI training cut-off-এর পর change miss করে।
  • 3. Vendor data feed: Confidential pricing AI-তে paste — competitor leak risk। Anonymize।
  • 4. Site safety neglect: AI office task speed-up করে, কিন্তু site supervision physical presence-এ। AI দিয়ে remote site management ভুল।
