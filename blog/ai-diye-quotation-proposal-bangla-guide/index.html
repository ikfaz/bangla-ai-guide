ভূমিকা

Freelancer ও ছোট business-এর জন্য quotation/proposal লেখা — সময়সাপেক্ষ কিন্তু critical। ভালো proposal = client win। AI এই কাজ ৩০ মিনিট থেকে ৫ মিনিটে আনতে পারে — quality compromise না করে।

Quotation vs Proposal — পার্থক্য

  • Quotation: Quick price quote, 1-2 page
  • Proposal: Comprehensive — problem, solution, timeline, price, terms
  • RFP response: Formal proposal answering specific RFP
  • Use: Quotation simple service, proposal complex project

Quotation — quick AI workflow

Master prompt:

  • "Write professional quotation for [service]. Client: [name/company]. Scope: [bullets]. Timeline: [duration]. Output:
    - Header with date + quote number
    - Itemized service breakdown with hours/rate
    - Subtotal, VAT (if applicable), total in BDT or USD
    - Validity period
    - Payment terms
    - Footer with signature line
    Tone: professional formal. Output in [Bangla/English]."

Proposal — 7-section structure

  • ১. Cover/Title: Project name, client, date
  • ২. Executive Summary: 1 page snapshot
  • ৩. Understanding of Need: show you "get it"
  • ৪. Proposed Solution: what + how + why
  • ৫. Timeline + Milestones: phased breakdown
  • ৬. Investment/Pricing: options A/B/C
  • ৭. About Us + Next Steps: credentials + CTA

AI prompt — full proposal

  • "Write 5-page business proposal for:
    SERVICE: [details]
    CLIENT: [info]
    BUDGET RANGE: [if known]
    TIMELINE: [duration]
    UNIQUE EDGE: [your differentiator]
    Include all 7 standard sections. Professional formal tone. Output in English. Estimated reading time: 7-10 minutes."

5টি Service-specific template

Web development

Sections: discovery, design, dev, QA, launch, support. Itemize page count, feature count.

Digital marketing

Channel breakdown, KPI targets, monthly retainer + ad spend.

Consulting

Diagnostic phase, recommendation, implementation. Hourly + project options.

Content creation

Article count, word count, revision rounds, deliverables.

SaaS implementation

Setup fee + monthly subscription + customization + training.

Pricing strategy (AI assist)

  • 3-tier offer: Always provides "good/better/best" — middle wins
  • Anchor high: First option premium → others feel reasonable
  • Bundle: 3 deliverables = $X (cheaper than each separate)
  • BDT vs USD: Local client BDT, international USD
  • Payment terms: 50% advance, 50% delivery standard

Visual design (free tools)

  • Canva: Proposal templates (paid client = paid Canva worth)
  • Notion: Live proposal links
  • Google Docs: Collaborative editing
  • Pandadoc: e-signature + tracking
  • PDF export: Final delivery

Win rate booster — psychology

  • Open with client's pain (proves you listened)
  • Use their words/terminology
  • Social proof: 2-3 brief case studies
  • Guarantee: money-back, satisfaction
  • Scarcity: "Q1 booking 2 spots left"
  • Easy yes: clear next step

BD context

  • Govt RFP: formal Bangla + English bilingual
  • Corporate (banks, telco): English standard
  • SME: Bangla casual mix OK
  • VAT: 7.5% standard mention
  • TDS: deduction note
  • Payment: bKash/bank transfer most common

উপসংহার

Proposal writing — sales skill। AI দিয়ে প্রতিটি draft ৫-১০ মিনিটে — but আপনার voice, client understanding, এবং follow-up = winning factor। Master prompt save করে রাখুন, ১০টি client win-এর পর niche-specific template build করবেন।
