৫. Transport — Air (US-Bangla/Novoair) vs Bus (Greenline/Shyamoli/St Martin)

Dhaka থেকে Cox's Bazar-এ তিনটি option:

  • Flight (recommended for family): US-Bangla, Novoair, Biman — Dhaka-CXB ৫০ মিনিট। Round-trip per person ৳7,000-12,000 (peak season higher)। Kids under 5 ৫০% discount। Mahmuda family: ৳7,200 × 2 adults + ৳3,600 × 2 kids = ৳21,600 one-way।
  • AC Bus (budget): Greenline, Shyamoli Paribahan, St Martin Paribahan, Hanif Enterprise। Dhaka-Cox's Bazar ৯-১১ ঘণ্টা। Per seat ৳1,200-2,500। Family of 4 round-trip ৳12,000-20,000।
  • Non-AC bus (lowest): ৳800-1,500 per seat। ১২+ ঘণ্টা — kid-unfriendly।
  • Private car: 8-seater Hiace ৳18K-25K one-way। Door-to-door comfort।

AI insight: Family with kids under 10 — flight strongly recommended। Bus 11 ঘণ্টা-এ kids exhausted, প্রথম দিন beach miss। Flight cost difference ৳15K-20K কিন্তু gain ১ extra usable day।

৬. Day trip planning — Inani, Himchori, Patenga, Maheshkhali

Cox's Bazar main beach-এর বাইরে আশেপাশের attraction-গুলো AI দিয়ে cluster করতে পারেন:

  • Inani Beach (35 কিমি south): Coral stones + cleaner water + Marine Drive scenic route। CNG ৳1,500-2,000 round-trip। Best 3-6 PM।
  • Himchori Waterfall (18 কিমি south): Hill view + small waterfall (monsoon best)। Combine with Inani same day।
  • Marine Drive (80 কিমি): পৃথিবীর দীর্ঘতম marine drive। Scooter rental ৳600/hour। Sunset point: Inani Royal beach।
  • Maheshkhali Island: Trawler ৳200 one-way। Adinath Temple, dry fish market, betel leaf garden। Half-day trip।
  • Patenga (Chittagong direction): Skip if short trip — far from Cox's Bazar।
  • Ramu Buddhist Temple (17 কিমি): Cultural day trip, kid-friendly।

৭. Saint Martin / Cheradwip extension — boat schedule + permit

Saint Martin — Bangladesh-এর একমাত্র coral island। Cox's Bazar-এ গেলে miss করার মতো নয়:

  • Operating season: October-March শুধু। Monsoon-এ ferry বন্ধ (rough sea, government ban)।
  • Ferry options from Cox's Bazar: St Martin Cruise, Karnafuli Express, Bay Cruiser। Departure: Nuniarchora ghat 7:00 AM, return 3:00 PM।
  • Ferry fare: Economy ৳1,200-1,800 round-trip। Business/AC ৳2,500-3,500।
  • Permit (২০২৫ থেকে required): Government tourism portal-এ pre-registration। Day-tour vs overnight separate quota।
  • Stay options: Blue Marine, Praasad Paradise St Martin, Sayman St Martin। ৳3,500-8,000/night।
  • Cheradwip (3 কিমি south): Low tide-এ পায়ে হেঁটে accessible। Pristine — no hotel, day trip only।

AI prompt: "Saint Martin overnight Feb 15-16, 2026, family 2+2 kids, permit process + ferry booking step-by-step + best hotel under ৳6K/night।"

৮. Food + safety + jellyfish/tide AI alerts

AI tool-এর underrated power — local safety + food recommendation:

  • Restaurant recommendations: Poushee (Bangladeshi), Mermaid Café (fusion, Pechardwip), Roof Top (seafood, Kolatoli), Salt n Pepper (family-friendly)। Avoid: overpriced hotel buffet-only।
  • Local seafood: Rupchanda fish fry, Korol fish curry, Coral fish। Fresh from Fisherman Wharf morning।
  • Jellyfish alert: March-May + October-November season। AI checks Bangladesh Met Office data। Lifeguard flag color: red = no swim।
  • Tide timing: High tide 6-hour cycle। Inani photo-spot only at low tide (coral visible)।
  • Sun safety for kids: 10 AM-3 PM avoid beach। SPF 50+ recommended।
  • Local SIM/internet: Grameenphone, Robi — strong network। Tourist eSIM also available।
