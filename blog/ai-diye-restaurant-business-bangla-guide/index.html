ভূমিকা

বাংলাদেশে রেস্তোরাঁ ব্যবসা — উচ্চ প্রতিযোগিতা, কম margin, দ্রুত-পরিবর্তনশীল trend। AI ৬টি জায়গায় বড় difference করতে পারে। প্রতিটির practical implementation।

১. Menu design ও photography

  • Midjourney/Flux: Food photography style image (real plate-এর জন্য reference)
  • Canva AI: Menu card design (multiple language)
  • ChatGPT: Item description writing — appetizing
  • Pebblely: Real photo → enhanced

২. Social media marketing

  • ChatGPT: Daily Facebook/Instagram post
  • Canva AI: Visual asset
  • Suno AI: Catchy jingle audio
  • Pika: Short video for Reels
  • ManyChat: Auto-reply DM (booking inquiry)

৩. Customer service

  • WhatsApp chatbot: Order taking, reservation
  • Foodpanda review reply: ChatGPT-এ "Reply this 3-star review professionally"
  • FAQ automation: Hours, menu, halal, parking
  • Multilingual: Bangla + English support

৪. Inventory & supply

  • ChatGPT + Excel sheet — consumption pattern analysis
  • Waste reduction calculation
  • Vendor price comparison
  • Demand forecast (festival, weekend)

৫. Review management

  • Foodpanda, Google Reviews: AI sentiment analysis
  • Pattern detection: "৫০% complaint delivery time-এ" — AI tells
  • Reply automation: Polite, customized
  • Insight to action: Kitchen workflow change

৬. Staff training

  • ChatGPT-এ training script generate
  • Role play (customer dispute) — AI as customer
  • Menu knowledge quiz
  • Bangla-English bilingual material

৭. POS + Data analytics

  • Toast, Square, Posist — AI features built-in
  • Sales pattern (peak hour, day, season)
  • Best/worst item analysis
  • Loyalty program optimization

বাংলাদেশি রেস্তোরাঁর জন্য ৩০-দিন AI adoption plan

  • Week 1: ChatGPT-এ daily social post + Canva menu
  • Week 2: WhatsApp chatbot setup (ManyChat free)
  • Week 3: Foodpanda review monitoring + reply automation
  • Week 4: Inventory analysis + supply optimization
