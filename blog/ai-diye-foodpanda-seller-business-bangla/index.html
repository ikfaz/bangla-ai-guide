প্রকাশিত: ২৬ মে, ২০২৬

ভূমিকা

Foodpanda বাংলাদেশের #1 food delivery — Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi-তে dominant। ১৫,০০০+ restaurant। প্রতিদিন হাজারো order। কিন্তু visibility, ranking, conversion — প্রতি seller-এর চ্যালেঞ্জ। AI এই কাজ ৭০% optimize করতে পারে।

১. Menu photography

  • Each item: professional-looking photo essential
  • AI tools: Photoroom (background clean), Pebblely (food scene)
  • Mobile shot → AI enhance → studio quality
  • Tip: consistent lighting + style entire menu
  • Cost saved: ৳20,000+ photographer fee

২. Menu description optimization

  • ChatGPT prompt: "Write appetizing 30-word description for [dish]. Highlight: taste, key ingredients, what makes it special. Bangla + English."
  • SEO: include search terms ("spicy biryani", "ঢাকার সেরা")
  • Allergen disclosure
  • Portion clarity
  • A/B test 2 versions

৩. Ranking + visibility hacks

  • Hours active: longer = better ranking
  • Acceptance rate: reject কম রাখুন
  • Preparation time: accurate estimate
  • Rating maintain: 4.5+ critical
  • Foodpanda Pro: badge boost (paid)
  • Featured collection: sales rep contact

৪. Customer review management

  • Daily monitor (Foodpanda app)
  • ChatGPT-এ negative review → professional Bangla reply draft
  • Common issues: late delivery, missing item, taste
  • Public apology + private resolve
  • Pattern detect: same complaint repeat = process fix

৫. Pricing strategy

  • Competition analysis (similar restaurant)
  • Combo deals: AI suggest profitable bundles
  • Time-based: lunch vs dinner pricing
  • Festival pricing: Eid, Pohela Boishakh
  • Loss-leader: cheap item to attract

৬. Marketing — direct + Foodpanda ads

  • Foodpanda Ads: $0.20-1/click, top placement
  • Foodpanda Voucher: 10-30% discount campaign
  • Pandapro members: reach + free delivery customers
  • Social media: drive to Foodpanda link
  • WhatsApp regular customers: direct order

৭. Operations efficiency

  • Order rush: AI sort prep priority
  • Inventory tracking
  • Wastage analysis
  • Staff scheduling
  • Daily P&L automate

৮. Multi-channel strategy

  • Foodpanda: convenience
  • HungryNaki: alternative
  • Direct WhatsApp: higher margin
  • Facebook page
  • Walk-in/dine-in
  • Catering

উপসংহার

Foodpanda — visibility = sales। AI দিয়ে photo, description, review reply, pricing — সব ৩-৫ ঘণ্টা-এর কাজ ৩০-৬০ মিনিটে। আজকেই top 5 dish-এর photo enhance করে দেখুন।
