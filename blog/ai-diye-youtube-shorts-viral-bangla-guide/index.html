প্রকাশিত: ২৬ মে, ২০২৬

ভূমিকা

YouTube Shorts এখন 2026-এর সবচেয়ে দ্রুত-বর্ধনশীল platform। বাংলাদেশ থেকে যেকোনো creator $0 budget-এ million-view potential রাখেন। AI এই game-কে আরো লেভেল করেছে — production cost-time প্রায় শূন্য।

Shorts algorithm — কী reward করে

  • Hook < 3s: viewer retain rate
  • Watch-through: 80%+ best
  • Replay: algorithm boost
  • Likes per view: 5%+ excellent
  • Saves/Shares: highest weight
  • Subscribe-from-Short: ultimate signal

Viral formula — proven structure

  • 0-3s: visual hook + provocative statement
  • 3-15s: setup the problem/curiosity
  • 15-45s: the payoff / "how to"
  • Last 3s: loop-able ending (replay boost)
  • Length: 30-50s sweet spot

AI script workflow

  • ChatGPT prompt: "Write 5 YouTube Shorts scripts on [niche]. Each: 60 sec max, hook in first 3s, conversational Bangla, end with loop"
  • Iterate: Best 1 from 5
  • Refine hook: "Make hook stronger — what would make Dhaka teenager stop scrolling?"
  • Translate variations: Bangla + Banglish version

Production (AI-accelerated)

  • Recording: Mobile vertical, no fancy gear
  • CapCut AI auto-edit: beat sync, captions
  • Auto-captions: Bangla 90%+ accuracy
  • B-roll: Pexels free stock
  • Music: YouTube Audio Library trending
  • AI voice over: ElevenLabs Bangla

Title + thumbnail

  • Title: 50-60 char, curiosity-driven
  • Avoid: "Part 1" (people skip if part 1 missed)
  • Bilingual mix: Bangla + English keywords
  • Custom thumbnail: only if 60s+; auto-frame OK for under

BD-specific viral niches (2026)

  • AI tutorials (this niche!)
  • Mobile photography tips
  • Cricket commentary/analysis
  • BD news daily breakdown
  • Food review
  • Hidden Dhaka spots
  • Bangla relationship advice
  • Study tips (BCS/SSC/HSC)
  • Freelancing income reveal
  • Tech reviews (BDT pricing)

Posting strategy

  • Frequency: 1 Shorts/day minimum first 90 days
  • Time: 6-9pm BD time (peak BD audience)
  • Series mindset: sequel-able Shorts (Part 2 trigger)
  • Cross-post: Reels + TikTok same content
  • Tag: #Shorts hashtag

Long video → Shorts (repurpose)

  • Opus Clip: 1-hour podcast → 10 viral Shorts
  • Selection: emotional peak, controversial take, "wait what" moments
  • Edit: caption highlight, zoom on face
  • Content type: education > entertainment for repurpose

Monetization

  • YouTube Shorts Fund: $100-10K based on performance
  • YPP eligible: 1K subs + 10M Shorts view in 90 days
  • Sponsorship: 10K+ subs
  • Affiliate links: bio + pinned comment
  • Direct to long-form: drive Shorts viewers to monetized 10+ min videos

Common ভুল

  • Slow hook (lose 90% in 3s)
  • Multiple ideas one Short
  • No captions (silent autoplay loses sound)
  • Vertical aspect wrong
  • Music too loud over voice
  • No CTA (subscribe/comment)

উপসংহার

YouTube Shorts বাংলাদেশের new gold rush। AI দিয়ে production barrier zero — শুধু consistency দরকার। ৯০ দিনের জন্য daily 1 Short commit করুন। যিনি ১০ দিনে quit করেন, তিনি ১১ তম দিনের viral hit miss করেন।
