Gemini vs ChatGPT — ২০২৬-এ কোনটি ভালো? (বাংলা Comparison)

ভূমিকা

Google Gemini এবং OpenAI ChatGPT — দুই tech giant-এর flagship AI। ২০২৬-এ এসে কোনটি এগিয়ে? Bangladeshi user-এর প্রেক্ষাপটে এই comparison।

Model versions (২০২৬-এর মাঝামাঝি)

  • Gemini: 2.0 Flash, 2.0 Pro, 2.5 Pro
  • ChatGPT: GPT-4o, GPT-4.5/5, o1, o3 (reasoning)

দাম

  • Gemini free: Generous — 2.0 Flash unlimited
  • Gemini Advanced: $২০/মাস ($২০/মাস includes Workspace integration)
  • ChatGPT free: GPT-4o mini unlimited; limited GPT-4o
  • ChatGPT Plus: $২০/মাস
  • ChatGPT Pro: $২০০/মাস (unlimited o1/o3)
  • Winner (free tier): Gemini
  • Winner (top-end): ChatGPT Pro

বাংলা ভাষা

  • Gemini: Native-quality Bangla — Google search context strong
  • ChatGPT: Excellent Bangla, slightly more nuanced creative writing
  • Winner: Tie — উভয়ই native-grade

Reasoning / math

  • Gemini 2.5 Pro: Strong (thinking mode)
  • ChatGPT o1/o3: Industry-leading
  • Winner: ChatGPT o3 narrowly

Coding

  • Gemini: Very good
  • ChatGPT (o1/GPT-5): Slightly better complex algorithm
  • Winner: ChatGPT slight edge

Multimodal (image, audio, video)

  • Gemini: Native multimodal from day one; video understanding
  • ChatGPT: Image yes; audio voice mode; video limited
  • Winner: Gemini for video; ChatGPT for voice quality

Tools/ecosystem

  • Gemini: Workspace integration (Docs, Gmail, Drive)
  • ChatGPT: Custom GPTs, Sora video, DALL-E, plugins
  • Winner: Use-case dependent

Long context

  • Gemini 2.5 Pro: 1M-2M tokens
  • ChatGPT: 128K (Plus), 1M (Enterprise)
  • Winner: Gemini for very long document

বাংলাদেশি user সুপারিশ

  • Student (free): Gemini
  • Content creator: ChatGPT
  • Developer: ChatGPT
  • Business analyst (long doc): Gemini
  • Workspace user: Gemini
  • Best workflow: দুটোই — Free Gemini + Plus ChatGPT

উপসংহার

দুটোই top-tier; "winner" আপনার use case। Free user হলে Gemini-তে best value; one paid subscription হলে ChatGPT Plus সবচেয়ে versatile।
