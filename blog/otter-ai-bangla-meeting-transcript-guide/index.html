মূল ফিচার

  • Real-time transcription: Live meeting-এ join করে instant transcript। ১-২ second delay only।
  • Speaker identification: Different speakers automatically labeled।
  • AI summary: Meeting শেষে auto-generated summary + key points + action items।
  • Otter Pilot: Meeting context-aware AI assistant — "What did Sarah commit to in the Q3 planning?" — accurate answer।
  • Search: Past meetings-এ keyword search। 6 months back-এ যে decision হয়েছিল — instantly findable।
  • Calendar sync: Google/Outlook calendar-এ meetings auto-join।
  • Integrations: Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, Zapier।
  • Slide capture: Screen-shared slides auto-extracted।

Setup (৫ মিনিটে)

  1. otter.ai → Sign up Google/Microsoft
  2. Settings → "Connect Calendar" → Google/Outlook auth
  3. "Otter Assistant" enable → calendar-এ যেকোনো meeting-এ auto-join
  4. Optional: Zoom Marketplace-এ Otter app install (deeper integration)
  5. First meeting test → transcript generate দেখুন

দাম

  • Basic (free): 300 minutes transcription/মাস, 30 min/meeting limit। Test করার জন্য।
  • Pro $১০/user/মাস: 1,200 min/মাস, 90 min/meeting, advanced features। Fariha-র team-এর tier।
  • Business $২০/user/মাস: 6,000 min/মাস, 4 hr/meeting, admin features।
  • Enterprise: Custom। SSO, dedicated support, compliance।
  • BD payment: USD card via bKash recharge।

বাংলাদেশি team-এর জন্য consideration

  • Bangla-English code-switching: BD professional meetings-এ frequent ("ভাই, এই deliverable-টা next sprint-এ push করব")। Otter primarily English-trained — Bangla word ৬৫-৭৫% accurate, English code-switch portion 90%+। Pure Bangla meeting-এ accuracy drops।
  • Accent: BD English accent handles decent। Indian English accent-এ slightly better।
  • Multi-speaker: 3-5 person meeting-এ speaker ID accurate। 8+ speakers-এ confused।
  • Privacy: Client/sensitive data-এ careful। Otter Business plan-এ data retention controls।
  • Bandwidth: Real-time-এ 50-100 kbps। BD residential internet sufficient।

Sokal 9 AM Engineering Standup (15 min)

Customer Interview (45 min)

5 customer interview/সপ্তাহ। প্রতিটার transcript + AI summary। Fariha-র research workflow:

  1. 5 interview শেষে Otter-এ "What are the common pain points across these 5 interviews?" ask
  2. AI cross-meeting analysis
  3. Pattern identify → product roadmap input
আগে ছিল ১৫-২০ ঘণ্টার manual analysis। এখন ১-২ ঘণ্টা।

Board Update (60 min)

CEO + investors-এর সঙ্গে monthly update। Otter Pilot use করে: "Did the CEO approve the Q3 hiring budget?" — instant answer। Past 6 months-এর decisions-এর knowledge base।
